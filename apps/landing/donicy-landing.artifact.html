Questions, answered

Everything you were about to ask

Do I need an accountant to file my GST returns? +

No. DONICY builds GSTR-1, 3B, 4 and 9 straight from your invoices and purchases, validates them against the GSTN schema, and exports portal-ready JSON. Many owners file themselves; if you work with a CA, they get their own login and a full audit trail.

Can I move my data over from Tally, Vyapar or Excel? +

Yes. Import clients, vendors and items via CSV templates, and bulk-upload bills. Our onboarding team helps Professional and Enterprise customers migrate historical data so you start with your ledgers intact.

Is my data secure? +

Every business’s data is isolated at the database level with row-level security, encrypted in transit and at rest. GST-portal and API credentials sit in an encrypted vault, and every create/edit/delete is written to an append-only audit log.

Which GST features are included? +

GST invoicing (CGST/SGST/IGST, HSN/SAC), e-Invoice (IRN) and e-Way Bill via a licensed GSP, GSTR-1/2B/3B/4/9, ITC and reverse-charge handling, and a compliance calendar with due dates and late-fee estimates.

Does it work on mobile and at the counter? +

Yes — the web app is responsive, there’s a companion mobile app for billing on the go, and USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners work out of the box for POS-style checkout.

What happens after the free trial? +

You get 14 days of the Professional plan, no card required. Pick a plan when you’re ready; if you don’t, your account simply pauses — your data stays safe and exportable.
